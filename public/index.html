THE RECOVERIT ENGINE

AI where judgment helps.
Rules where money is at risk.

RecoverIT does not send every decision to an AI model. Deterministic checks handle identity, evidence presence, provider events, duplicate protection and action safety. AI is used for the parts that benefit from reasoning: reconstructing messy stories, comparing conflicting signals, explaining uncertainty and choosing the next useful question.

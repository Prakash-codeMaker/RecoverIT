# RecoverIT Demo Guide

Use one concrete recovery case instead of a feature tour.

## Recommended scenario

**₹18,500 refund dispute**

The merchant says the refund was processed. The user cannot see the money in the original payment account.

## Demo story

1. Start with the user's messy description.
2. Show RecoverIT extracting the payment facts it can support.
3. Add payment proof.
4. Show evidence verification completing.
5. Show the case workflow move into investigation.
6. Show the selected recovery route and exact external contact preview.
7. Pause on the explicit **Accept & send now** gate.
8. Send through the supported path or show the guided official route.
9. Capture the organization response.
10. Show the same case moving into monitoring and resolution.

## What to emphasize verbally

> AI handles the messy interpretation. The deterministic workflow decides what RecoverIT is allowed to do.

Then show an exception immediately after the happy path: rejected evidence, missing payment context, AI unavailability or a provider-response request. This proves that the product is designed for reality rather than only the success path.

# RecoverIT product demo

The strongest demo is one real-looking synthetic case, shown end to end.

## Story

Use a synthetic ₹18,500 refund-not-received case:

1. Report the problem naturally.
2. Attach payment/refund evidence.
3. Show evidence verification.
4. Show investigation and the persistent next step.
5. Show the recovery route appear only after required checks pass.
6. Show the exact destination, evidence and prepared message.
7. Approve with **Accept & send now**.
8. Capture the organization response.
9. Continue through requested evidence or refund monitoring.
10. Confirm the outcome.

The demo should prove that RecoverIT is a product that continues a case, not a chat interface that stops after an AI response.

A DIFFERENT WAY TO REPORT A PAYMENT PROBLEM

Don't fill out a form.
Just tell us what happened.

Speak naturally or type the story. RecoverIT builds the case from your words, then asks only for the proof that can actually help establish what happened.

STEP 1 · YOUR STORY

Start with the story, not the fields.

Press and speak

Tell us what you paid for, what happened, and what the merchant/payment provider told you.

STEP 2 · PROOF

Show us what you have.

Upload payment receipts, bank statements, refund messages, order screenshots, emails, or other relevant proof. You can add more later.

WHY THIS MATTERS

RecoverIT will check whether the evidence is consistent with your story, whether the timeline makes sense, and whether there is provider-side evidence available. It will never call a document “genuine” merely because it is uploaded.

CASE CREATED

Now RecoverIT investigates.

We're reconstructing the payment story, checking the evidence you've supplied, and looking for conflicts. Stay here — the guidance will update as new information is available.
